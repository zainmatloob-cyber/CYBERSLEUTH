# Real-world cut-off for model-only technique suggestions

Generated by `scripts/choose_real_world_threshold.py` on the **development split** of the second CISA set (9 advisories). The test split was not read.

**Rule (fixed before the first run):** among the candidates 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, choose the lowest decision score at which at least 50% of model-only suggestions match a technique CISA cites (parent level). If none does, choose none.

| Cut-off | Suggestions kept | Agree with CISA | Agreement | 95% interval* | Advisories with a suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| -0.647 (current training threshold) | 358 | 24 | 0.07 | 0.04–0.10 | 9 |

| 0.50 | 30 | 14 | 0.47 | 0.30–0.64 | 8 |
| 0.75 | 17 | 10 | 0.59 | 0.36–0.78 | 7 |

\* Wilson interval treating suggestions as independent. They are not (suggestions from one advisory are correlated), so the true uncertainty is wider.

## Decision

**Chosen cut-off: 0.75.** It is the lowest candidate that reaches 50% agreement on the development split. It is set as `model_only_min_score` in `config/technique_mapping.json` and is tested once on the test split.

## Caveats

* Agreement is a lower bound on precision: CISA's technique lists are incomplete.
* The candidate values came from the first set's error analysis, which is why the first set can't be used to test them.
* Nine advisories is a small development set; the test split gives the number to report.
